# Candidate validation

The official AP0.6.8 Options Creator constructs exactly Vanilla, Balanced,
Compatible in that order and exports clean YAML with Vanilla as default. IDs
remain Vanilla=0, Compatible=1 and Balanced=2. The removed pool/range selectors
are unregistered, not hidden choices. AP's ordinary unknown-key warning is not
sufficient for removed selections; a narrow game-specific parse guard rejects them.

Seven paired official generation/solver cases cover default, Balanced, Compatible,
Mixed Fortresses, By Type, weighted YAML and consumable Hammers. Equivalent supported
settings preserve item placements, assignments and world/multiworld RNG in this
bounded comparison. Four complete ROM pairs match the accepted gameplay baseline.
Contract version/shape, source provenance, resource identities and archive bytes
intentionally differ. No general cross-release exact-seed guarantee is claimed.

All 53 certified original stages remain, including the earlier twelve. Balanced
enforces source-world distance at most one. Compatible retains only certified
technical edges and demonstrates distant-world assignments; difficulty annotations
do not restrict its graph. Required exits, routing, entry and softlock constraints
remain. Special stages and reviewed Mixed Fortresses pass generation/ROM checks.

The runtime is checked with a fresh isolated official AP/BizHawk lifecycle and
coinstalled independent SMB3 proof of concept. Test selections, navigation and
goal approach are assisted; no human full-campaign/controller certification is
inferred. Wider boundary tests, source-only builds and inherited native evidence
remain scoped by their recorded source/resource identities. Publication is gated
by NOTICE.md, independently of technical build and gameplay validation.
